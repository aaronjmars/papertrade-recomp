The current 284 active-selector rows and detailed behavior maps cover the newest connected suite only. The capture closes the direct-transaction nonce gap for this one deployer, but does not prove there were no other team deployers, internal creations, reachable dynamic targets, or economically relevant balances. Before extending any whole-protocol security conclusion, classify other balance and claim types, other token holdings, the meaning of internal accounting, upgrade histories, remaining multisig and library behavior, and the active older Exchange variants.
