# Older Exchange account census at block 48,902,346

The [event capture](../../scripts/atlas_older_event_census.py) queries every log emitted by each older Exchange proxy from its creation block through the audit pin. It uses contiguous archive `eth_getLogs` windows, splitting a window when the provider rejects its response size or returns 10,000 logs. The saved [first-set](../../artifacts/deployer_scope/older_exchange_0_events.json.gz) and [second-set](../../artifacts/deployer_scope/older_exchange_1_events.json.gz) records contain 1,216,528 and 142,814 logs. Every successful subwindow has fewer than 10,000 logs, every log is tied to its Exchange and block range, and transaction plus log indexes are unique. The pinned block hash matches the manifest.

The [balance verifier](../../scripts/atlas_older_account_balances.py) extracts 160-bit address-shaped indexed words from those logs, then adds every older factory user, all 59 known direct creations, and the deployer as candidates. An address-shaped word is a lead, not proof of an event field's ABI type. Archive `balances(address)` calls at the pin test every candidate. For every nonzero getter, a separate raw storage read at `keccak256(address, 9)` returns the same value. Every nonzero account was also present in an indexed Exchange event.

The [first-set](../../artifacts/deployer_scope/older_exchange_0_balances.json.gz) and [second-set](../../artifacts/deployer_scope/older_exchange_1_balances.json.gz) captures preserve each queried address, its candidate sources, getter output, storage slot, and the raw storage output for nonzero accounts. The factory-only sample previously represented 3,296,777.828011776131754231 and 432,874.813948011436749202 internal units. The additional event-derived accounts account for the rest of each global getter. One first-set and one second-set Staking proxy also hold nonzero Exchange balance and appear in the event-derived accounts.

This exact reconciliation is strong evidence that the captured addresses account for `totalBalances()` at the pin. It is not a proof that every mapping slot is enumerable from events or that `totalBalances()` includes locked margin, queued payouts, reward claims, or every other economic obligation. The separate [position](older_positions.md) and [queue](older_queue_claims.md) reconciliations now check locked margin, global queues, and observed queue users; it does not prove that these internal units are redeemable USDC. The older Exchange Core spot balances in the [separate public API capture](deployer_scope.md) are much smaller; those balances are not a sufficient solvency comparison without the complete accounting and settlement semantics. The [withdrawal census](older_withdrawals.md) proves delivery for 33 recorded historical withdrawals, not a general redemption guarantee for these balances.

Run `python3 scripts/atlas_older_event_census.py` and `python3 scripts/atlas_older_account_balances.py` to verify the saved evidence offline. To refresh it, set `PAPERTRADE_ARCHIVE_RPC` to an archive HyperEVM RPC URL, run `python3 scripts/atlas_older_event_census.py --capture-set 0` and `--capture-set 1`, then run `python3 scripts/atlas_older_account_balances.py --capture-set 0` and `--capture-set 1`.
