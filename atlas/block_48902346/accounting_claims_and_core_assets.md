# Claims and Core assets at block 48,902,346

The pinned HyperEVM block was mined at 2026-10-18 21:02:21 UTC. The three active Exchange suites have an exact internal treasury identity at that block. The newest Exchange's public HyperCore spot ledger reconstructs to **64,930,263.85521554 USDC**, exactly **1 USDC more** than its EVM treasury value of **64,930,262.85521554 internal USD units**. This is a comparison of one Core account and one EVM book field, not a whole-protocol solvency proof.

## Pinned Exchange books

[`atlas_all_suite_claims.py`](../../scripts/atlas_all_suite_claims.py) checks raw Exchange, Tokenomics, and Staking storage, the pinned account and position censuses, Staking's PAPER token balance, and the common Staking implementation runtime. The exact identity in each suite is `treasury = ordinary balances + locked margin + total queued + staker fee reserve + developer fee reserve + signed tracked LP`. Both `total queued` and the side bucket are zero in all three suites. **Correction (2026-10-23):** the side bucket is deliberately not a term in this identity. It is an earmark of the spendable LP bankroll rather than a claim, so adding it double counts whenever it is nonzero. At block 48,173,889, where a real close wrote `400296219202209635129` into Exchange slot 27, the archive reads `treasury - reserved` = `400296219202209635129` = `trackedLp 377424392655154164362 + totalQueued 22871826547055470767`, while adding the side bucket would give `800592438404419270258`; the pinned reconciliation above only holds because the side bucket reads zero there. See [SQ-L2-03](live2_sequences.md) in the sequence ledger, which verifies the corrected form against live state and the live replay. ## Core comparison and limit

[`atlas_newest_core_assets.py`](../../scripts/atlas_newest_core_assets.py) saves every returned `userNonFundingLedgerUpdates` entry for the newest Exchange from the start of the public ledger through the last millisecond of the pin second. The 81,842 captured entries are all USDC spot transfers. The fetch splits time ranges until every response has fewer than 500 entries, checks gap-free time coverage and ordering, and sums incoming transfer amounts and outgoing amounts plus fees in eight-decimal raw units. Some API amount strings have floating point tails smaller than a quarter of one raw unit; the verifier rounds them to the nearest raw unit and rejects larger tails.

A further 10,519 public ledger entries from immediately after the pin through the independent spot account observation at 2026-10-20 15:44:27 to 15:44:28 UTC replay to **50,301,262.51179847 USDC**, exactly matching that observed current spot balance. Its hold is zero, and the separately observed current perp account has zero withdrawable and no positions. This validates the ledger arithmetic against a live observation. It does not prove that the API omitted no historical entry or that Core held the reconstructed amount in cryptographically pinned state. The public [`spotClearinghouseState` API](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint/spot) has no historical block parameter; [HyperEVM's default RPC](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/hyperevm/json-rpc) does not offer historical state reads. The [public ledger API](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/Hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint) is the basis of this historical reconstruction.

For the two earlier Exchanges, the [separate Core capture](deployer_scope.md) found no post-pin public ledger entries through its 2026-10-19 observation. Their observed Core spot balances of **0.156176** and **0.003269 USDC** are therefore pinned inferences under the same ledger-completeness assumption. These are far smaller than their EVM treasury fields. The same-time comparison does **not** establish that the older books are redeemable one for one or that these Exchange Core accounts are the full custody perimeter. A full solvency conclusion would require identifying every asset-holding Core and EVM account and obligation, the contractual meaning and withdrawal rights of the older internal balances and tracked LP, and historical custody evidence independent of the public ledger. Those are the exact remaining gaps; no surplus or deficit is asserted for the protocol as a whole.
